Kufuta taarifa zako — Mzunguko

Mzunguko (na LeonTechForge) haina akaunti wala seva inayohifadhi kumbukumbu zako. Taarifa zako zote ziko kwenye simu yako tu, hivyo unazifuta wewe mwenyewe:

  1. Fungua Mzunguko → Zaidi → Mipangilio ya Faragha.
  2. Gusa Futa taarifa zote na uthibitishe. Kila kitu kinafutwa mara moja: hedhi, kumbukumbu za kila siku, ujauzito, vikumbusho, PIN na mipangilio.
  3. Au ondoa programu kwenye simu — taarifa zake zote huondoka pamoja nayo.

Nakala (backup) au ripoti ulizotuma mwenyewe mahali pengine hazifutwi na programu; zifute pale ulipozihifadhi. Kwa usajili wa Plus, usitishe kwenye Google Play → Malipo na usajili.

Deleting your data — Mzunguko

Mzunguko (by LeonTechForge) has no account and no server storing your logs. All your data lives on your phone only, so you delete it yourself:

  1. Open Mzunguko → More → Privacy settings.
  2. Tap Delete all data and confirm. Everything is erased immediately: periods, daily logs, pregnancy data, reminders, PIN and settings.
  3. Or uninstall the app — all of its data is removed with it.

Backups or reports you sent elsewhere yourself aren’t removed by the app; delete them where you saved them. To end a Plus subscription, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
