Sera ya Faragha ya Mzunguko

Imesasishwa: 17 Oktoba 2026 · Mchapishaji: LeonTechForge

Kwa ufupi

Mzunguko ni programu ya kufuatilia hedhi, rutuba, ujauzito na afya ya mwanamke. Taarifa zako za afya zinabaki kwenye simu yako. Hakuna akaunti, hatuna seva ya kuhifadhi kumbukumbu zako, na hatuuzi wala kushiriki taarifa zako za afya na mtu yeyote.

Taarifa unazoandika

Tarehe za hedhi, dalili, hisia, ute, joto la mwili, vipimo vya LH na mimba, uzito, maji, dawa, miadi, maelezo ya ujauzito, jina na tarehe ya kuzaliwa (si lazima). Zote huhifadhiwa ndani ya simu kwenye hifadhidata ya programu na hutumika tu kukuonyesha makadirio, takwimu na vikumbusho. Hazitumwi kwetu wala kwa watangazaji.

Kufunga programu na hali ya siri

Unaweza kufunga programu kwa PIN au alama ya kidole, kuficha programu kwenye orodha ya programu za karibuni, kubadilisha ikoni kuwa "Kalenda", na kutumia arifa zisizoonyesha maelezo ya afya. PIN huhifadhiwa kwa njia ya siri (hash) ndani ya simu.

Kumshirikisha mpenzi

Ukichagua "Mshirikishe mpenzi", programu hutengeneza kiungo chenye taarifa ulizochagua tu (k.m. awamu ya mzunguko na tarehe ya hedhi ijayo). Taarifa hizo ziko ndani ya sehemu ya kiungo baada ya alama "#", ambayo vivinjari havitumi kwa seva — ukurasa huzisoma kwenye simu ya anayefungua tu. Hatuhifadhi viungo hivi. Mtu yeyote mwenye kiungo anaweza kukiona, kwa hivyo kishiriki na unayemwamini tu.

Uliza Mzunguko

Maswali unayoandika hujibiwa ndani ya simu kutoka orodha ya majibu iliyomo kwenye programu. Hayatumwi popote.

Matangazo

Toleo la bure lina matangazo ya Google AdMob. Matangazo yote huombwa kama yasiyo ya kibinafsi (hayalengi kwa kutumia historia yako), na hayaonyeshwi kwenye skrini zenye taarifa zako za afya. Google inaweza kukusanya kitambulisho cha matangazo cha kifaa, anwani ya IP na taarifa za jinsi matangazo yanavyoonyeshwa ili kuyaonyesha, kupima na kuzuia udanganyifu. Taarifa zako za afya hazishirikiwi na Google. Pale sheria inapohitaji (k.m. Ulaya), programu huomba ridhaa yako kwanza, na unaweza kubadilisha chaguo lako kwenye Mipangilio → Faragha ya matangazo. Maelezo zaidi: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Mzunguko Plus (malipo)

Malipo hufanywa kupitia Google Play. Hatupokei wala kuhifadhi maelezo ya kadi au pesa ya simu. Programu huuliza Google Play tu kama akaunti yako ina Plus.

Nakala na kuhamisha

Unaweza kutengeneza nakala iliyosimbwa (encrypted), ripoti ya PDF kwa daktari au faili za CSV. Faili hizi huundwa kwenye simu na kwenda tu mahali unapozituma wewe mwenyewe.

Kufuta taarifa

Zaidi → Mipangilio ya Faragha → Futa taarifa zote hufuta kila kitu mara moja. Kuondoa programu pia hufuta taarifa zake. Tazama ukurasa wa kufuta taarifa.

Watoto

Mzunguko imekusudiwa watu wazima wenye umri wa miaka 18 na zaidi.

Si ushauri wa kitabibu

Makadirio yote ni makadirio tu. Mzunguko si njia ya uzazi wa mpango na haichukui nafasi ya mtoa huduma ya afya.

Mabadiliko

Tukibadilisha sera hii, tutasasisha tarehe iliyo juu.

Mzunguko Privacy Policy

Updated: 17 October 2026 · Publisher: LeonTechForge

In short

Mzunguko is a period, fertility, pregnancy and women’s health tracker. Your health data stays on your phone. There is no account, we have no server that stores your logs, and we never sell or share your health data with anyone.

Data you enter

Period dates, symptoms, moods, discharge, temperature, LH and pregnancy tests, weight, water, medicines, appointments, pregnancy details, and optionally your name and date of birth. All of it is stored on the device in the app’s database and used only to show you predictions, insights and reminders. It is never sent to us or to advertisers.

App lock and discreet mode

You can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint, hide it from the recent-apps screen, change its icon to a neutral “Kalenda”, and use notifications that don’t reveal health details. The PIN is stored on the device only as a secure hash.

Sharing with a partner

If you use “Share with partner”, the app creates a link containing only what you chose (for example your cycle phase and next period date). That information sits in the part of the link after “#”, which browsers never send to a server — the page decodes it on the opening phone only. We don’t store these links. Anyone with the link can see it, so share it only with someone you trust.

Ask Mzunguko

Questions you type are answered on the device from an answer bank inside the app. They are not sent anywhere.

Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Every ad is requested as non-personalised (not targeted using your history), and ads are not shown on screens with your health data. Google may collect the device’s advertising ID, IP address and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Your health data is not shared with Google. Where the law requires it (e.g. the EEA/UK), the app asks for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options. More: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Mzunguko Plus (purchases)

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive or store card or mobile-money details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has Plus.

Backups and exports

You can create an encrypted backup, a PDF report for your doctor or CSV files. They are created on the device and only go where you send them.

Deleting your data

More → Privacy settings → Delete all data erases everything immediately. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. See the data deletion page.

Children

Mzunguko is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

All dates are estimates. Mzunguko is not a method of contraception and does not replace a health worker.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
